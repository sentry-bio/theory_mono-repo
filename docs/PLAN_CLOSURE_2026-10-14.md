## The spine (settled 2026-10-14)
Both papers study one inequality, the lower side of the relational obligation,
`D⁻¹·d_S(u,v) − K ≤ d_M(addr u, addr v)`, together with ε-separation (Base) which is the same inequality at
d_S = 2. The upper side is never binding in a bounded-diameter host and is not the Kesten–Stigum line.
Paper I asks which hosts honour the inequality at exponential census rate; Paper II asks how long a random
writer honours it and what the reader can recover. Kesten–Stigum (second moment, optimal reader) does not
reduce to the inequality and is kept as the separate ceiling for the best reader.
