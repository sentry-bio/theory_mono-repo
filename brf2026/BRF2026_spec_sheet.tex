\textbf{What the datum is.} A frozen, reproducible reference frame for cellular genomic diversity: any genome, named or not, receives an \emph{identity coordinate} relative to a catalogue of reference genomes, and lineages receive \emph{routes} on a separate genealogical backbone. The frame rests on two layers with different evidentiary standards: unconditional theorems (Paper~I, machine-checked in Lean~4) that say what each layer can carry, and pre-registered measurements (Paper~II and the datum's own validation ledger) that say how far each instrument reaches. Nothing learned enters the foundation layer; no later realisation moves an existing coordinate.

\textbf{The one inequality.} Histories that are far apart must stay far apart in the record, $D^{-1}d_S(u,v)-K\le d_M(\mathrm{addr}\,u,\mathrm{addr}\,v)$. At the distance of sisters it is the resolution condition that gives identity; at the distance of deep cousins it is the condition that keeps genealogy. Every band below is a statement about how long some instrument honours it.

\textbf{Why the frame has layers.} A fixed-window statistic of a genome (any $k$-mer composition) has at most $(L+2)^{a^k}$ distinguishable states against $a^L$ ordered ones (Paper~I, \texttt{Heredity.lean}); and over bounded-valence sources, relational capacity is bounded by packing entropy at every scale (the coarse packing converse, \texttt{CoarseConverse.lean}), which is zero for a Euclidean coordinate space. So no composition map can carry deep branching, and a frame for cellular life must have an identity layer and a separate genealogy layer. The architecture is a corollary, not a design preference.

\textbf{Open items carried by this realisation (not hidden in a frontier list).} (1)~The 4-mer map's genealogical horizon, predicted near $0.2$ substitutions per site from the neutral channel count, is not yet measured as registered. (2)~Archaea: the composition horizon failed as registered; the archaeal band carries the weaker label until a reader is found. (3)~The sextant's depth law is confirmed in the model; on real alignments its finite-length prediction and reader ordering are confirmed (PREREG23b) and its depth exponents are not, blocked by a reader-dependent floor on short internal edges that more sequence does not remove; the third design is specified (Paper~II, \S7.2). (4)~Organelles and viruses are outside the reference set.

\textbf{Realisation rule.} A realisation adds genomes and coordinates; it never changes the recipe, the fingerprint, or an existing distance. Each realisation re-issues this sheet with its validation numbers and its open items. The learned instruments (survey map, gate, novelty, chimera, operations) sit outside the foundation layer and may change between realisations without touching it.
